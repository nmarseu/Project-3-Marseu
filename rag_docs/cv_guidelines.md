# CV Guidelines

Rules the agent uses to critique a CV against a specific job posting and suggest improvements.

## Principles
- Tailor, don't rewrite. Suggest specific bullet changes for the target role; keep the candidate's structure unless it is clearly broken.
- Every suggestion must use facts from the candidate's achievement bank. Never add skills, numbers or experiences the candidate does not have.
- Recruiters skim a CV in seconds. The most relevant experience for the target role should be visible in the top third of page one.

## Bullet points
- Start each bullet with a strong action verb (led, built, launched, scaled, reduced, designed).
- Use the pattern: action + what + measurable result. Example: "Scaled a new production facility from 20K to 120K+ units/week."
- Put the number early in the bullet when there is one.
- One idea per bullet, ideally one to two lines.
- Remove duties without results ("responsible for...") or turn them into outcomes.
- Avoid vague words: "various", "several", "helped with", "involved in".

## Matching the job posting
- Mirror the posting's key terms where they are true for the candidate (e.g. if the posting says "go-to-market", use that phrase instead of a synonym). This also helps ATS keyword screening.
- Reorder bullets within a role so the ones most relevant to the posting come first.
- Must-have requirements from the posting should each be backed by at least one bullet. If one is missing, flag it as a gap and suggest how to address it honestly (e.g. an adjacent experience, or mentioning it in the cover letter).

## Structure and format
- Order: contact line, (optional) 2–3 line profile summary tailored to the role, experience, education, skills/languages.
- Keep it to one page for under ~10 years of experience, two pages maximum otherwise.
- Consistent date formats, consistent punctuation, no typos or broken brackets.
- A short profile summary is worth adding when the candidate is changing industry or function, to frame the story.
- List tools and technical skills explicitly if the role is technical or AI-related.

## What the agent should output
- 3–6 specific suggestions, each with: the current bullet (or "missing"), the suggested rewrite, and why it matters for this role.
- A short list of gaps between the posting's must-haves and the CV.
- Any formatting or consistency issues spotted.
