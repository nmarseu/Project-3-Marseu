How it works

Three inputs, one email.

Runs on its own once you submit. Usually done in two to three minutes.

1

Share the job

Paste the posting link. If the page can't be read, paste the description instead and the agent uses that.

2

Upload your CV

Add a PDF CV and, optionally, results that aren't on it yet: team sizes, savings, growth. Pick English or German.

3

Get your pack

The agent researches, analyses, writes and fact-checks, then emails you the full report.

What's in the pack

Everything you'd prep by hand, in one report.

Company brief
Business model, current situation, recent news with dates, and what the role really needs.
Fit analysis
A score out of 100 and every requirement marked strong, partial or gap, with how to address each gap.
CV fixes
Your weakest bullets, rewritten with real numbers. Before and after, side by side.
Cover letter
250–350 words that open with something specific about the company. No "I am excited to apply".
Interview prep
Company talking points, likely questions, STAR stories mapped to them, and sharp questions to ask the interviewer.

Built to be trusted

It never claims what it can't prove.

AI cover letters often invent experience. This agent is designed not to.

Only your facts

Every claim about you comes from your CV or the achievements you added. Nothing else.

Self-checking

A second AI reviews the report against the sources. If something doesn't hold up, the report is rewritten.

Nothing stored

Your CV and achievements are used for this one report and not saved to the knowledge base.

Honest about weak matches

Below a fit score of 50 you get a short note on the gaps and what would close them, not a pack for a role you're unlikely to get.
